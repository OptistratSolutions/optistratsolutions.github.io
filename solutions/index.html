03 / Automation, monitoring & edge systems

Practical connected control where a large automation platform is not justified.

Compact systems can monitor, record, alert and control at the edge—while connecting operational information to the people who need it.

  • Equipment status, runtime, temperature, level and alarm monitoring
  • Door, pump, motor, environmental and other sensor states
  • Timers, sequencing, interlocks, relays and actuator control
  • Local/offline data capture with controlled synchronisation
  • Browser dashboards, alerts and rules-based operational actions
✓

Appropriate scope matters. These are practical small-to-medium monitoring and control systems. Large PLC, safety-critical or SCADA engineering is coordinated with appropriately qualified specialist partners where required.

Technology with an operational purpose

The platform is selected around the job—not the other way around.

Hardware names describe implementation options. The actual product is better control, better information and faster operational response.

Remote equipment monitoring

Capture operational state, runtime, environment, alarms and other useful conditions from field equipment.

Embedded control systems

Sequencing, timers, interlocks, relay and actuator control for compact special-purpose applications.

Raspberry Pi edge systems

Microcontroller systems

Web-connected monitoring

Automated alerts & actions
