About OptiStrat Solutions

Operational understanding with project discipline and technical depth.

OptiStrat Solutions is a Gauteng-based operations, project-management and systems-development company built to bring structure, accountability and visibility to complex work.

The company

We manage the operation and build the control around it.

OptiStrat Solutions works with industrial, technical, property and project-based organisations dealing with multiple stakeholders, operational complexity and limited internal project capacity.

Our role is to establish the structure, coordinate execution and give management a reliable view of what is happening. Where the operation needs better tools, we design the dashboard, workflow, data capture or practical automation system that supports that control.

This is not generic IT support, handyman work or disconnected software development. Every service in the spotlight supports the same outcome: controlled execution and better operational decisions.

Founder-led accountability

Led by Jennifer van Loggerenberg.

Jennifer brings more than ten years of experience across project and operations coordination in technical and industrial environments.

Her background spans mechatronics, electronics, industrial automation, engineering operations, procurement, maintenance and multi-disciplinary project delivery. This combination allows OptiStrat Solutions to communicate effectively with management, technical teams, suppliers and contractors without losing sight of the commercial objective.

Clients have direct access to the person accountable for scope, decisions and delivery—keeping communication clear and responsibility visible.

Operating principles

Clear scope. Visible ownership. Practical execution.

Good control is not bureaucracy. It is the minimum structure required to move complex work reliably.

Understand before prescribing

We assess the operation, constraints and risk before selecting a tool or solution.

One accountable control point

Stakeholders know where coordination, escalation and reporting responsibility sits.

Decisions over activity

Reporting is built around what management needs to decide and what the team needs to do.

Technology with a job

Appropriate technical scope

Specialist and regulated work is delivered with qualified partners where required.

Continuous improvement

Once data exists, the operation can be refined based on evidence instead of assumption.

Partner network

Coordination responsibility stays clear—even when specialist work is required.

OptiStrat Solutions remains responsible for integrating the scope, schedule, communication, documentation and reporting into the broader project.
